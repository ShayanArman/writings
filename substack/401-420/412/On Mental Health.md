On Mental Health
Words are powerful

The Problem with ‘mental health’ is that we label many problems as mental in nature when they are not.

If you got no friends and your sad that’s emotional health.

If you have no life purpose that’s spiritual health.

If you got no money that’s financial health.

If you hit your head and fell - well you’re just plain stupid.
